Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains information handling for the public SpinDownGames™ website, Spinarium accounts, and the contact links it provides.

What the website collects

The website provides card, community, and event information. It currently has no checkout, contact forms, or mailing-list signup. Spinarium saves account information when you sign up. Creating an account does not establish administrator access or grant collectible ownership.

The dice roller keeps its recent rolls in the open page’s memory. That history resets when the page reloads and is not sent to us or saved in browser storage. Fonts, card images, event flyers, and the coin model are served with the website.

Spinarium accounts

The account service receives your email-shaped login identifier, display name, and password over HTTPS through Cloudflare Workers. The login identifier is trimmed and lowercased, stored in Cloudflare D1, and is not verified as an email address you control. An unverified or non-deliverable address may be used to sign up; it does not establish your identity or grant administrator authority.

The service processes your password to create or check a salted, slow password hash. It stores the hash rather than your plaintext password. D1 also stores an application account ID, display name, account dates and status, the last successful sign-in when recorded, server session records, and security records used to limit repeated requests. Collection records link to the application account. Trusted administrators can review account identifiers, profiles, access roles and status, session counts, and collection records to provide support and manage access. Account and catalog changes record the administrator, affected account or item, time, and relevant changes for accountability. Administrative account changes also record the supplied reason. New accounts start with no owned Veilings.

Automatic email-based password recovery is not available. After checking an account support request, an administrator can provide a temporary, single-use password-reset link through an agreed private contact channel. The service stores a digest of the reset credential and its expiry, and records reset issuance and completion. Keep this link private: anyone holding a valid link can set a new password for its account. Administrators cannot view your current password. Account access depends on your password and current server session. Session expiry limits access; it does not promise immediate deletion of stored records. To ask about account information or request its correction or deletion, use the private contact options below.

Hosting and technical information

Cloudflare manages the domain’s DNS and hosts the website and account endpoints through Workers. Cloudflare D1 stores Spinarium account, session, collection, and security records. Cloudflare processes requests to deliver the site and its services. Current public artwork is served with the website; private R2 artwork storage is not enabled. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for its own information practices.

A GitHub Pages copy is retained as fallback hosting. If you visit a version served by GitHub Pages, GitHub states that it records visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made without a GitHub account. GitHub controls its hosting logs and their retention; this website does not provide us with those logs.

Read GitHub’s hosting disclosure and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its processing practices and privacy options.

Cookies, browser storage, and tracking

We have not added analytics, advertising pixels, or advertising tracking cookies. Spinarium saves whether its welcome introduction has finished in this browser’s local storage, so later visits can bypass it. Clearing that site storage resets the introduction. This preference is local to the browser and is separate from your server account records.

Spinarium uses an essential session cookie that expires after eight hours. This cookie is sent over HTTPS and cannot be read by page scripts. Signing out revokes the Spinarium session. Introduction completion remains browser-local; it is not stored as a per-account D1 preference. Browser cookie controls can prevent secure sign-in from working.

The website’s code does not track you across other websites or change its behavior in response to a browser’s Do Not Track signal.

This describes the website’s own features. Hosting and the external services you choose to visit have their own information practices.

Discord, Instagram, and messages

Our community links take you to Discord or Instagram. They are links, not embedded feeds or tracking widgets. When you follow them, the platform handles information under its own policy: Discord Privacy Policy or Instagram Privacy Policy.

If you message our team, we can see the profile information, message content, and attachments you make available to us and use that information to respond to your inquiry. This happens through the platform, not a form on this website. Posts in public community channels can also be seen by other members; avoid posting sensitive information there.

Event directions

The directions button opens Google Maps with the event’s address. This website does not request your location or embed a map. If you use Google Maps, Google handles your information under the Google Privacy Policy, and its settings control any location access.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse the public website without creating an account or joining a community. Personal Spinarium collection access requires secure sign-in. Use your browser’s controls to clear local site storage, and the relevant provider’s controls to manage information you share there. GitHub, Cloudflare, Discord, and Instagram operate their own systems and information practices.

To ask about Spinarium account information or information you have sent to the SpinDownGames™ team, or to request access, correction, or deletion, contact us through the links below. We may need to verify that a request relates to you. The rights and exceptions that apply depend on applicable law; requests about a provider’s logs or your platform account should also go to that provider.

Children’s information

If a parent or guardian believes a child has shared personal information with our team or used an enabled account service, please contact us through the private options below. Separate platforms have their own age requirements and privacy rules.
