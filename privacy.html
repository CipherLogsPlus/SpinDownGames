Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains information handling for the public SpinDownGames™ website, Spinarium, and the contact links it provides. Secure Spinarium accounts are being prepared; the public experience currently remains a temporary preview.

What the website collects

The website provides card, community, and event information. It currently has no checkout, contact forms, or mailing-list signup. Spinarium’s temporary preview login does not create a real account, establish administrator access, or grant collectible ownership.

The dice roller keeps its recent rolls in the open page’s memory. That history resets when the page reloads and is not sent to us or saved in browser storage. Fonts, card images, event flyers, and the coin model are served with the website.

Spinarium accounts being prepared

Account registration and secure sign-in are not yet active on the public preview. The prepared account service uses Auth0 to handle email and password entry, email verification, and password recovery. When enabled, those details are processed by Auth0 under Okta’s Privacy Policy. Spinarium’s account service does not receive or store your password.

The prepared Spinarium service checks your verified email and identity before granting a session. It stores an application account ID, the identity provider’s account identifier, display name, account dates, account status, and server session records in Cloudflare D1. Email is checked during sign-in. The application database has no separate email field, but the identity provider’s display name may contain an email address. Collection records link to your application account; trusted administrators’ catalog changes are recorded for accountability.

Session records and temporary sign-in attempts support authentication and security. Request identifiers are also used to limit repeated sign-in requests. Session expiry limits access; it does not promise immediate deletion of stored records. To ask about account information or request its correction or deletion, use the private contact options below.

Hosting and technical information

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub states that it records visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made without a GitHub account. GitHub controls its hosting logs and their retention; this website does not provide us with those logs.

Read GitHub’s hosting disclosure and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its processing practices and privacy options.

Cloudflare manages the domain’s DNS. Cloudflare Workers, D1, and R2 are being prepared for Spinarium’s account and collection services and artwork. When those services are enabled, Cloudflare processes requests and stores the application records and artwork needed to provide them. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for its own information practices.

Cookies, browser storage, and tracking

We have not added analytics, advertising pixels, or advertising tracking cookies. Spinarium saves whether its preview welcome introduction has finished in this browser’s local storage, so later visits can bypass it. Clearing that site storage resets the introduction. Preview login state stays in the open page’s memory and resets on reload; it is not a real account session.

When secure accounts are enabled, the prepared service uses an essential session cookie that expires after eight hours and a temporary sign-in cookie that expires after ten minutes. These cookies are sent over HTTPS and cannot be read by page scripts. Signing out revokes the Spinarium session; an Auth0 session may remain. Browser cookie controls can prevent secure sign-in from working.

The website’s code does not track you across other websites or change its behavior in response to a browser’s Do Not Track signal.

This describes the website’s own features. Hosting and the external services you choose to visit have their own information practices.

Discord, Instagram, and messages

Our community links take you to Discord or Instagram. They are links, not embedded feeds or tracking widgets. When you follow them, the platform handles information under its own policy: Discord Privacy Policy or Instagram Privacy Policy.

If you message our team, we can see the profile information, message content, and attachments you make available to us and use that information to respond to your inquiry. This happens through the platform, not a form on this website. Posts in public community channels can also be seen by other members; avoid posting sensitive information there.

Event directions

The directions button opens Google Maps with the event’s address. This website does not request your location or embed a map. If you use Google Maps, Google handles your information under the Google Privacy Policy, and its settings control any location access.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse the public website without creating an account or joining a community. When enabled, personal Spinarium collection access requires secure sign-in. Use your browser’s controls to clear local site storage, and the relevant provider’s controls to manage information you share there. GitHub, Cloudflare, Auth0, Discord, and Instagram operate their own systems and information practices.

To ask about Spinarium account information or information you have sent to the SpinDownGames™ team, or to request access, correction, or deletion, contact us through the links below. We may need to verify that a request relates to you. The rights and exceptions that apply depend on applicable law; requests about a provider’s logs or your platform account should also go to that provider.

Children’s information

If a parent or guardian believes a child has shared personal information with our team or used an enabled account service, please contact us through the private options below. Separate platforms have their own age requirements and privacy rules.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its information practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
