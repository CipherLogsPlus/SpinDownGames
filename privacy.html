Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains information handling for the public SpinDownGames website and the contact links it provides.

What the website collects

The website provides card, community, and event information. It currently has no customer accounts, checkout, contact forms, or mailing-list signup. There is no visitor account database maintained by this website.

The dice roller keeps its recent rolls in the open page’s memory. That history resets when the page reloads and is not sent to us or saved in browser storage. Fonts, card images, event flyers, and the coin model are served with the website.

Hosting and technical information

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub states that it records visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made without a GitHub account. GitHub controls its hosting logs and their retention; this website does not provide us with those logs.

Read GitHub’s hosting disclosure and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its processing practices and privacy options.

Cookies and tracking

We have not added analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or persistent browser storage to this website. Its code does not track you across other websites or change its behavior in response to a browser’s Do Not Track signal.

This describes the website’s own features. Hosting and the external services you choose to visit have their own information practices.

Discord, Instagram, and messages

Our community links take you to Discord or Instagram. They are links, not embedded feeds or tracking widgets. When you follow them, the platform handles information under its own policy: Discord Privacy Policy or Instagram Privacy Policy.

If you message our team, we can see the profile information, message content, and attachments you make available to us and use that information to respond to your inquiry. This happens through the platform, not a form on this website. Posts in public community channels can also be seen by other members; avoid posting sensitive information there.

Event directions

The directions button opens Google Maps with the event’s address. This website does not request your location or embed a map. If you use Google Maps, Google handles your information under the Google Privacy Policy, and its settings control any location access.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse the website without creating an account or joining a community. Use the relevant platform’s controls to manage information you share there. GitHub, Discord, and Instagram control their own systems and retention settings.

To ask about information you have sent to the SpinDownGames team, or to request access, correction, or deletion, contact us through the links below. We may need to verify that a request relates to you. The rights and exceptions that apply depend on applicable law; requests about a provider’s logs or your platform account should also go to that provider.

Children’s information

The website does not offer forms or accounts to collect children’s personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child has shared personal information with our team, please contact us. Separate platforms have their own age requirements and privacy rules.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its information practices change. The date above identifies the current version.

Contact SpinDownGames

For privacy questions or requests, message @spindowngamingco on Instagram (opens in a new tab) or contact the team through the SpinDown Games Discord community (opens in a new tab). Ask for a private conversation rather than posting personal details in a public channel.
